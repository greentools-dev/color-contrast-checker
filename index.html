Accessibility Guide & FAQ

What is the formula for relative luminance?

Per W3C specifications, relative luminance linearly maps sRGB values after gamma expansion: L = 0.2126 * R + 0.7152 * G + 0.0722 * B. The contrast ratio is then (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L1 is the lighter color.

What constitutes "Large Text" under WCAG?

Large text is defined as 18pt (typically 24px) or larger, or 14pt (typically 18.66px) or larger if bold (font-weight 700+). Because larger glyph strokes are easier to resolve, the required contrast threshold drops from 4.5:1 to 3.0:1.

How does the auto-fix feature work?

When you click "Auto-Adjust", the algorithm converts your foreground color to HSL space and iteratively modifies its lightness channel in step increments until the calculated contrast against your background meets or exceeds 4.5:1.

Can I test transparent colors?

WCAG contrast calculation assumes opaque colors rendered on solid surfaces. When designing transparent or translucent overlays, composite them against their underlying surface before testing.
